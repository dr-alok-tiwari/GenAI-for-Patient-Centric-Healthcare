# Medical training assets

- `synthetic_chest_xray.png` was generated specifically for this workshop with OpenAI image generation on 2026-07-28. It is fully synthetic, contains no patient data, and has no diagnostic ground truth.
- `synthetic_prescription_01.png` through `synthetic_prescription_06.png` are code-generated mock prescriptions containing fictional names and values drawn from `data/prescription_cases.csv`.
- All assets are for education only. They are not clinical evidence and must not be used for diagnosis, prescribing, or patient care.
