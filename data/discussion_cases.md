# Discussion Cases

## Case 1: Patient stops medication after online AI advice
A patient with hypertension says an AI chatbot told them lifestyle change is enough and asks to stop amlodipine. Discuss counselling, shared decision-making, safety-netting, and documentation.

Discussion lens: patient centricity, safety, verification, accountability, documentation, and escalation.

## Case 2: AI differential diagnosis overconfidence
A resident uses an AI tool and anchors on a rare diagnosis. Discuss red flags, base rates, missed common diagnoses, and senior review.

Discussion lens: patient centricity, safety, verification, accountability, documentation, and escalation.

## Case 3: Off-label pharma query
A physician asks whether a drug can be used off-label in pregnancy. Discuss medical information boundaries, balanced evidence, escalation, and PV obligations.

Discussion lens: patient centricity, safety, verification, accountability, documentation, and escalation.

## Case 4: Hospital triage chatbot
A hospital plans a public chatbot for appointment triage. Discuss consent, emergency escalation, language, privacy, and audit.

Discussion lens: patient centricity, safety, verification, accountability, documentation, and escalation.

## Case 5: AI-assisted literature review
A researcher asks AI to write an entire review. Discuss search transparency, citation verification, author accountability, and reproducibility.

Discussion lens: patient centricity, safety, verification, accountability, documentation, and escalation.

## Case 6: Patient data pasted into public AI
A doctor pastes discharge notes into a free tool. Discuss PHI risk, de-identification, institutional policy, and safer workflows.

Discussion lens: patient centricity, safety, verification, accountability, documentation, and escalation.
