# Asset Attribution

## Synthetic medical assets

- `assets/medical/synthetic_chest_xray.png` — generated specifically for this workshop with OpenAI image generation on 2026-07-28. Fully synthetic; contains no patient data; has no diagnostic ground truth.
- `assets/medical/synthetic_prescription_01.png` through `synthetic_prescription_06.png` — code-generated mock prescriptions based on fictional records in `data/prescription_cases.csv`.

These assets are for education only and are not clinical evidence.
